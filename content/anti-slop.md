# Anti-AI-Slop Design Standard

## Prohibited defaults
- Purple-to-blue gradients without a product-specific reason.
- Glassmorphism as a universal surface treatment.
- Emoji as product icons.
- Identical card grids where content relationships do not justify cards.
- Generic copy (“Unlock the power of…”).
- Centered hero plus three-column feature grid as an automatic template.
- Inter plus rounded-2xl on everything.
- Fake testimonials, fabricated logos/metrics, false scarcity or unsupported claims.
- Decorative charts, meaningless badges, random blobs, excessive shadows and gratuitous motion.
- Demo data presented as real customer data.

## Required evidence for major choices
1. Which user need/task does this support?
2. What evidence or established pattern supports it?
3. What alternative was considered?
4. What changes for responsive and accessibility contexts?
5. What happens when data is missing, loading, invalid or unavailable?

## Visual direction
Define a product point of view (e.g. dense/utilitarian, calm/editorial, expressive/creator-focused, precise/technical). Translate it into type roles, semantic color, spacing, density, surfaces, imagery and motion tokens.

## Quality bar
Realistic content; coherent hierarchy; meaningful empty/loading/error/success states; task-fit components; color never sole signal; readable typography under zoom; purposeful motion respecting reduced-motion; references used as inspiration, not copied.

## Review
Could this belong to any unrelated SaaS product? Are flourishes useful? Is proof fabricated? Is the action clear without decoration? Are all states designed?
